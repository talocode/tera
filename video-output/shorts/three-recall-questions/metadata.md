# Turn One Topic Into 3 Recall Questions

## Brief

- Audience: students who want a short, repeatable way to study a concept without rereading.
- Failure: studying stays passive because answers remain visible in the notes.
- Useful outcome: turn one topic into three recall questions and make missed answers the next review list.
- Proof: topic -> three questions -> closed-note answer -> missed-answer review queue.

## Voiceover

Here is how to turn one study topic into three recall questions.

Start with the topic: how does photosynthesis work?

Then ask three things: what is the goal, what are the inputs, and what comes out?

Close your notes. Answer each question from memory.

Now check the gaps. Those missed answers are your revision list.

Do the same tomorrow with the same three questions.
